Other gaps worth flagging

Even taken on its own, the LOI is missing terms a family (or their attorney) will ask about.

Gap

No loan terms anywhere

Every version of this deal runs on "my investor/lender," but no interest rate, term, or security is ever stated — not for the $300k mortgage in the LOI, not for the private financing in Offer 1 or 2. This is the one place the family's own attorney will push hardest, especially given the whole pitch is built on not repeating what happened to other families.

Gap

Offer 2's numbers don't resolve

"Pay an immediate $60,000 … take $250,000 out of Woodside" — is the $60k part of the $250k, or on top of it? As written, it can be read either way, and the two readings put very different amounts of debt on the house.

Gap

Woodside as collateral isn't disclosed

Pulling $250,000 in equity to fund a DC flip means Woodside stands behind that loan. If the flip underperforms, the family's home is what's exposed. The document never says this plainly — it should, given the story you lead with is about protecting people from exactly that outcome.

Gap

No financing or inspection contingency

Section 3 commits to a $300,000 mortgage; Section 4 is titled "Inspection and Due Diligence" but waives all contingencies. If the mortgage doesn't come through, there's no stated way out.
